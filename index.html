Why can’t they just…?

How American politics works, in nine short chapters

By Jens Kjeldgaard-ChristiansenDepartment of English and American Studies Center Aarhus, Aarhus University

Nine short chapters on how the United States is governed: the three branches, the elections that fill them, and the states and the Constitution around them. You learn by tapping, dragging and guessing, with real maps, votes and documents.

The idea behind all nine: the system splits power on purpose. Most laws need the House, the Senate and the president to agree, and each is elected separately. So big change is slow, and it usually takes broad agreement or a rare moment when one party holds all three.

About the facts
